Version 01.01	42 minutes dev	4 minutes CPU	4 minutes test		50 minutes

A few days ago, I discovered an interesting puzzle game called The Middle. It
is a guessing game. The game has a huge database of words - these are unknown
to players. There is a word that's chosen as the (secret) answer. At the start
of the game, you are given one word that is 10,000 words alphabetically before
the answer. You are given one word that is 10,000 words after the answer. You
guess in the middle, trying to find that answer. It is fun.
I will play. But it got me curious: can an app help? The database isn't known.
But as you guess words, you learn there positions relative to each other.
With some care, you can grow your own database. As you plan, the app could
give you advice that could help you find an answer more quickly.
I created a description of a utility app for this to Claude. It is a utility
used with that game. In the The Middle Helper, it asks you the top and bottom
works. It recommends a guess based on the word is the middle (as close as it 
can). After the game, you can put the words and offsets used during that game.
This creates a subset of what the game itself has. It has to build slowly. I
started with one game. From there, I can add more where the words overlap.
This is useful now. Need to add functionality to improve.

Version 01.04	0 minutes dev	0 minutes CPU	10 minutes test		1 hour 25

I have a theory for why words would get out-of-order in the database. The
root cause is that UI text says the words at the start that are 10,000 
words away from  the puzzle's answer... but they really aren't exactly 
10,000 words away. The game appear to take all the words of the English
language into the game. Many are esoteric. In this game, rare words are
never the words at the edges or the actually word to guess. My theory:
they couldn't have it be exactly 10,000 words away. The keep track of the
common words, and picks... something, that is far away, but does not 
need to be exactly 10,000 steps.
My new way of recording the game is that I do not enter those edge words
in on every problem. The other guesses, they always return a specific
number. Maybe that is accurate? Let's see.
I stored all the games that I did originally, removing those end words.
Thus far, there have not been any errors. Maybe this does fix the problem?
Let's see.
